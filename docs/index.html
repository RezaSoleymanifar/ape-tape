ape‑tape

An hourly record of the ApeWisdom boards. They publish the current snapshot and no history, so this reads it every hour and keeps what it read. Metadata about Reddit mention counts, on a schedule.

What this is, exactly

What ApeWisdom counts

How often each ticker is mentioned and upvoted on nine finance subreddits: all-stocks, all-crypto, wallstreetbets, stocks, cryptocurrency, options, investing, stockmarket, pennystocks. Seven numbers per ticker — rank, mentions, upvotes, and the same figures from 24 hours ago.

What it is not

Not Reddit data. There are no posts, comments, users, subreddit statistics or topics here, and none are stored. A symbol nobody names is invisible, and a thread with a thousand comments about the market that mentions no ticker does not exist to it.

Who matched the tickers

They did, and the method is unpublished. Common-word symbols like OPEN, IT and ALL, and private companies like SPCX, do appear. We record what the board said rather than correcting it, because a correction we invented would be the thing you could not audit later.
