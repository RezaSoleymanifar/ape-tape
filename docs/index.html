crowd‑tape

An hourly recording of what retail forums are talking about. The source publishes only the present — there is no history endpoint, and anyone selling you years of it re-scored old posts with today's model. So this tape starts the hour it was begun, is never back-filled, and every row carries the minute we read it.

How the crowd moves

Rank of the loudest tickers, hour by hour. Lines that dive are attention arriving; lines that climb are a story going quiet. This is the view that cannot be reconstructed after the fact — it exists only because someone was recording.

Statistics computed from the public ApeWisdom board and recorded hourly by crowd-tape. The stored series is derived — ranks, deltas, velocities, churn — not a copy of anyone's table.
